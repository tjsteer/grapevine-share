Your job search, with friends.

Grapevine finds openings that fit you every morning, tracks what you apply for, and lets friends pass each other leads and intros. Install it and any job page is two taps away: Share, then Grapevine.

Installed. Grapevine is on your home screen and in your share menu. Try it: open any job page, tap Share, and pick Grapevine.
You’re in an in-app browser, which can’t install apps. Tap ⋮ at the top right and choose Open in Chrome, then come back to this page.
  1. Tap ⋮ at the top right of Chrome, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. Open Grapevine from the new icon and sign in with your Claude account. A free account works, and you don’t need the Claude app.
  3. On any job page, tap Share and pick Grapevine. It lands in your tracker, filled in.
  1. In Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen. That gives you the Grapevine icon.
  2. Open Grapevine from the icon and sign in with your Claude account. A free account works, and you don’t need the Claude app.
  3. To share links in, open Grapevine’s profile and follow Add from anywhere. It takes about a minute to set up the iPhone Shortcut.
